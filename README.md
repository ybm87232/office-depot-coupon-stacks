## The fine print that quietly voids most Office Depot coupons

Office Depot's official coupon terms exclude more categories than almost any other major retailer's. If your cart is full of excluded items, a sitewide-looking "20% off" code simply won't apply, and you'll waste a checkout cycle figuring that out.

Here's the official exclusion list that applies to threshold discounts ("$10 off $50"-style offers) unless the coupon explicitly says otherwise:

- Clearance and closing-store merchandise
- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- Technology and consumer electronics, accessories, and software
- Furniture and seating
- Cleaning, breakroom, facility, and safety products
- Ink and toner cartridges
- Postage and shipping services
- Appliances, subscriptions, third-party marketplace items, and pallet-quantity purchases

Other terms worth knowing before you build a cart:

- **Minimums are calculated after discounts and before tax.** If a coupon needs a $150 qualifying purchase, your post-coupon, pre-tax total has to clear it.
- Coupons can't be combined with Rewards Member or Select Member pricing, or with other promotional pricing.
- One free-gift coupon per household or business, and free gifts are invalid in Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands.
- Free-gift offers additionally exclude purchases of stamps, gift cards, ink and toner, and products from HP, Samsung, Canon, Epson, Brother, Lexmark, and Microsoft Surface.

That last exclusion matters for one of the most common shopping patterns: buying a printer. A coupon discount on the printer itself is possible (recent offers have included 40% off HP Smart Tank models), but free-gift promos won't apply to HP purchases, and ink almost never counts toward any threshold.

Business accounts have their own wrinkle. Business Select members can't participate in the regular Office Depot Rewards program, and Business Select discounts don't combine with promotional pricing or coupons — Office Depot just applies whichever gives you the better value. If you run your purchases through a Business Select account, the stacking strategies below mostly don't apply to you.
